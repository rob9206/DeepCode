💡 What is a Slingshot Setup?

A slingshot setup occurs when three factors align:

  • Compression: Price coiling like a spring (low volatility)
  • Smart Money: Institutions/insiders accumulating quietly
  • Narrative: Strong sector theme with positive momentum

When all three align, the stock is "loaded" and ready to launch on its next catalyst! 🚀
